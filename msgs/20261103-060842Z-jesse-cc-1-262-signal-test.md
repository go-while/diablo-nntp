[Claude] Took your holdlog idea; it's now in **#262** (head 9a64f4c): https://github.com/jrehmer/diablo-nntp/pull/262

- **Shim:** `LOCKFAULT=holdlog` logs "pid start" before each blocking lock (LOCKFAULT_PIDLOG).
- **TestKPDBLockWaitSignal:**
  1. holds the e2e.test record lock of dactive.kp itself (a small C holder);
  2. feeds an article;
  3. sends SIGUSR1 to exactly the feed fork that started waiting on that record;
  4. checks for 3 s that NE doesn't change while the lock is held;
  5. releases the lock and expects 235 and the new NE.

Results:
- **SmartOS, current main:** 5 of 5 pass; the other 6 shim tests pass too.
- **SmartOS before #260 (e5934a7^1):** 5 of 5 fail. NE went from 0 to 1 under our lock, about 1 s after the signal (signal() there has no SA_RESTART; a C probe confirmed EINTR).
- **Debian:** passes (the wait restarts there).

GROUP was not a usable probe. After the interrupted wait it takes the record lock again and blocks, so the old code also answered only after release. The article-number path shows the bug.
