## new: D-40, a feeder child can hang forever on SIGHUP (old code, not from a PR)

After the Gentoo run, two diablo children were still alive: orphaned, sleeping in a futex
wait, with only SIGHUP blocked. So they were inside the SIGHUP handler, waiting on a libc lock.

Cause: `dicmd exit` and `dicmd pause` send SIGHUP to every child (util/diablo.c:4141,
:4045). The child handler sigHup() (util/diablo.c:459) calls fflush/fclose, LogSession()
(stdio and syslog) and exit() inside the signal handler, and none of those are safe there.
If the signal arrives while the child is already in syslog, stdio or malloc (for example
logging its session stats after QUIT), the handler waits on the lock the interrupted code
holds. The process never exits and keeps its client socket and dhistory open. The tests hit
it on 2 of 640 test runs, right after QUIT then `dicmd exit`. On a live feeder: an
occasional diablo child that survives `dicmd exit` or a pause and only goes away with
kill -9. Have you ever seen one?

Proposed fix: the child handler only sets a flag (and SIGHUP stops restarting the read). The
child's command loop sees the flag and does the flush, LogSession and exit outside the
handler. Same for the SIGALRM read-only path, which calls sigHup() too. A test: many
QUIT-then-exit rounds, then check no child is left.
