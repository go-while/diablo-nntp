ReadListLine() semantics: a 4096-byte buffer filled with getc(), up to 4095 bytes; it stops after storing an LF. Returns 0 when nothing could be read (EOF, or a read error: ferror is not checked, as with fgets before), 1 for a line ending in LF or a partial last line at EOF. Buffer full: it peeks one byte; LF or EOF means the line fits (that LF is eaten, not stored); anything else is read to the next LF in O(1) memory, then "Line over 4095 bytes - skipping : <first word, up to 40 bytes, sanitized>" is printed and it returns -1; the loops skip the line and do not count it. What fits: LF lines up to 4095 content bytes; CR LF lines up to 4094 (F1); a last line at EOF without a line end up to 4095. CRs are stripped by the parsers' strtok as before; the ".\r\n" terminator, LF-only ".", "..group", empty and whitespace-only lines behave as on main.
