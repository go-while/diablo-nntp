One thing for #272, or a follow-up, that fits Jesse's "config switches yes, design no" rule: FreeBSD (and BSDI) also set
```
USE_KP_RW_MAP  USE_PCOMMIT_SHM  USE_PCOMMIT_RW_MAP  USE_SPAM_SHM  USE_SPAM_RW_MAP
```
With USE_KP_RW_MAP, KPDB writes same-length updates and the '-' delete mark straight into the shared map (lib/kpdb.c ~806 and ~1050) instead of pwrite(). On OpenBSD that is exactly what keeps the other processes' maps current, so TestKPDBConcurrentUpdate and some of the active-file reader timeouts may go away with no code change. The precommit and spam-cache switches do the same for their caches. History has no such switch: its slot update stays a pwrite, so the history lookups (TestPeerIHAVE, TestDexpire STAT) will likely still fail on OpenBSD. That part would be the design item Jesse ruled out.
