Your long-name question: the header field oh_Gname is 256 bytes (dreaderd/defs.h:721). Every writer zeroes the header and copies at most 255 bytes, so the stored name is always NUL-terminated (dreaderd group.c:1338, dgrpctl.c:1098, dcancel.c:890; dexpireover sets the NUL explicitly). Names of 256 characters or more are refused by the newgroup control (control.c:1691) and by dgrpctl newgroup (dgrpctl.c:775). So strncmp(..., 256) stops at the NUL and behaves exactly like strcmp for every name a writer can store.
