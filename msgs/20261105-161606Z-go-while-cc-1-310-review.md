Nits, your call:
1. A Message-ID header with a comment or trailing text ("<a@b> (x)") fails the hash and is counted as "hash mismatch", which reads like a spool problem. Cutting msgid after the first '>' before hhash would store such articles too, and keep the count meaning what it says.
2. GroupsMatch() copies GroupPats into pats[4096] and a longer -g is cut silently; a length check at option parsing (refuse, as Usage) would be clearer. The same goes for a Newsgroups value beyond ngbuf/HEADMAX, which counts as "not in -g groups"; that is fine, but maybe worth one man-page line.
3. Compression is decided by today's dspool.ctl (SpoolCompressed(H_SPOOL(h.exp))), as diablo's readers do; a spool whose compression setting changed since its articles were stored would be read wrong. Also one man-page line, no code.
