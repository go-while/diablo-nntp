One thing before a go, the cost of the lookup:
- FindCanceledMsg() walks the group's over index from NE down to NB, up to ov_MaxArts entries, synchronously in the reader process. It holds the active record with KP_LOCK_RO for the whole walk.
- For cancels that was fine, because they are rare. Now any reader can start it, and an unknown id ("XHDR Subject <x@y>", in a loop) always walks the whole group.
- In a big binary group that stalls every other connection of that reader process, and holds the record lock the feed side needs to update NE.
Suggestions, your pick:
(a) Read NB/NE, unlock, then walk without the lock. The walk only reads the mmapped index, and an article added meanwhile is simply not found.
(b) Bound the walk for the reader path, e.g. the last N articles, with an option or a fixed 100k. Past the bound the answer is 430. Leave the cancel path as it is.
With (a), and either (b) or a note in ISSUES/changes/ that the lookup costs O(articles in the group), it's a go from me.
