Two more, plus an arm64 find on #227. Still nothing in lib/parsedate.c.

- #227 got a second commit. Its new localspool test failed on the
  ubuntu-24.04-arm runner, and that was a real bug: the localspool parser
  states (dfa_InHdr, dfa_LF) hold negative values but were plain char, which
  is unsigned on arm64/ppc/s390x. So reading articles via localspool= has
  never worked on those platforms. Now signed char; reproduced and verified
  on x86 with -funsigned-char.

- #230 lib: path_* settings expand into PATH_MAX buffers instead of 128
  bytes, so long paths are no longer silently cut. This is the root cause of
  the TestAdmHistbackup note in #227-#229 (it only failed because our
  checkout sat under a long path). Bounds the copies the old cap was
  protecting; two of them (notify socket name, FeedCommit) could already
  overflow a stack buffer from long local config. New long-path e2e test.
  Full suite green.

- #231 diablo: the feeder writes an uncompressed article body and its 1-byte
  terminator in one write: 900 -> 600 spool writes for 300 articles.
  Companion to #229.

All independent; merge in any order after our go per PR.
