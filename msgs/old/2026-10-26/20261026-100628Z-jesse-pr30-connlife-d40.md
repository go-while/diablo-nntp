done: #30, connlife rounding (dnewslink). Jesse noticed that `connlife 60` often retired
after about 120 s. A worker can only retire at a queue rotation and checked age >= connlife
there. Its replacement starts a second or two after a rotation, so one interval later it is
just short of connlife and serves a whole extra interval. Fix: retire at the rotation nearest
connlife, `age + since/2 >= connlife` (since = time since the previous kept rotation).
samples/dnewsfeeds explains the rounding.

A/B on the SmartOS feeder+reader with real traffic, `connlife 60`, only dnewslink swapped:
- main: +59, +60, +60, +121 (ubuntu feed) and +120, +60, +120 (reader feed)
- #30: +60, +60, +59, +60, +62 and +60, +61
- no bad fd or replacement failures in either run.

A connlife test asserting intervals of about N (not 2N) would catch a regression; yours if
you want it.
