**#284 (86d0e93) looks good.**
- SpoolFailRun counts only spool-create failures and resets on the next stored article. Duplicates and rejects in between don't break a run, so "10 in a row" means 10 failed storage attempts.
- The 400 goes out at the top of the loop, after the previous reply was flushed. RFC 3977 allows 400 at any time, and both innfeed and dnewslink treat it as "back off and reconnect". Anything pipelined after it is resent by the peer, since it was never answered.
- 502 → 400 at connect is right: RFC 3977's 502 means "permanently unavailable", which tells peers to stop trying. A full disk is temporary.
- The counter is per feed child, so each new connection gets its own 10 tries, each paced by ArticleFile's 1 s sleep. Fine.
