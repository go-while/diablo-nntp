[Claude] Thanks for the correction on #267 (ENOENT exits as before) and for the numbers from your logs.

#266 is merged; main is 80f8827. dreaderd now answers a login flood with a quick 400 instead of leaving clients in the accept backlog.
- The listener stays on while the DNS workers are busy, and the backlog is SOMAXCONN.
- A new connection is queued only while its estimated wait is within half of `readerdnswait` (new option, default 5 s; it was a fixed 10). Otherwise it gets the existing "Server too busy" 400 at once.
- New option `readerdnsqueue` (default 256), plus fd headroom.
- AUTHINFO logins have their own queue, served first. The old 4*readerdns+4 cap that gave logged-in users 400 on AUTHINFO PASS is gone.
- More than 200 refusals in 100 ms pause the listener for the rest of that 100 ms.
- A handed-back connection whose fd was dropped at the descriptor limit is now released. Before, it stayed counted in Act= until a restart; that leak was on main too. An ETOOMANYREFS fd pass now answers 400 instead of killing the reader.
- At startup dreaderd raises its soft RLIMIT_NOFILE up to the hard limit, at most FD_SETSIZE.
- Benchmark, 3x overload at 75 ms DNS: greeting p99 27 s with 389 resets before, 2.7 s with no resets now.

Two things for your boxes:
- If you run dreaderd under a low `ulimit -n`, the startup log now says what limit it raised to.
- Untested on FreeBSD: from the source, a descriptor shortage in recvmsg() may drop the whole message instead of setting MSG_CTRUNC, so the old leak could remain there. If you get a chance, run TestReaderDNSAuthFdLimit there under `limits -n 100`; it needs prlimit, though, so it may need a FreeBSD variant.
