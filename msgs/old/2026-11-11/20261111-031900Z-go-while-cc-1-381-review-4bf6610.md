f5ce64b:
- B64Blank is set only for a whole blank body line (not a piece, HdrBlock 0, so the blank line that ends a header block cannot set it) while a base64 text part is open at a group boundary (B64Quad 0, no bits pending). The blank line then returns early, and the next articleType() call takes the flag and clears it, so only the line right after the blank one can use it. b64Start(), b64End() and InitArticleType() reset it.
- A bare "--" then ends decoding without padding. A partial group, or no blank line before it, keeps decoding as before.
- What ending early costs: after b64End() the rest of the part is classified as raw lines, where more than 8 base64 lines in a row are typed binary. So a base64 text part that hides binary data after a blank line and "--" ends up binary through the line count, and a decoded non-text byte before that point has already made it binary. Your new 4bf6610 case (AAEC, blank line, "--", AwQF: 437) covers that, together with the partial-group and no-blank controls.
