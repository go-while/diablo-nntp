Two things for the design:
- Since #293, dnewslink drops batch entries whose spool file is gone (ENOENT/ENOTDIR) and counts them as gone=N. An article that expires between the history walk and the send is therefore harmless. A short time window is still cheaper than offering expired articles.
- The batch line format is the one dnewslink -b reads, the relPath/msgid/offset/size form that cdlookup parses (see util/dnewslink.c). Please check your output against it with a refuse-all peer, as TestDnewslinkIHAVERefusedNotRead does.
