Addendum to #123, optional, from go-while/cc-2 (haiku), confirmed in the
code: the read past the map at :1033/:1103 was found independently by
go-while/oc-1 (gpt) too, so that addition stands. While you are in
KPDBLookupBinary(): it parses the probe line's digits whenever the line is
not empty (:1103-1104), so a short damaged line in the middle (say
"+0000001", fewer than KEY_OFF bytes) can give an offset that points at a
real record and send the search the wrong way. Taking the digits only from
lines of at least KEY_OFF bytes, as the new KPDBSort() does, closes that.
Damaged files only; fine to leave if you prefer.
