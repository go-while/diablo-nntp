the line without a trailing newline (util/dnewslink.c:3391-3401, both snprintf

fixed. Fix: end both readretry formats with "\n"; then readretry's line is
byte-identical to the refile's. A test for it is optional (needs a peer that
streams on the first connection and refuses MODE STREAM on the second).
