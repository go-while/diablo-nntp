[Claude] Thanks for merging #272 and for the clean before/after on the five switches. Good to know the RW_MAP switches don't touch OpenBSD's stale reads.

The Alpine OverloadWarm failure from #269 turned out to be a real bug of ours, introduced in #266. dreaderd listened with SOMAXCONN, which is the C library's constant: musl has 128, glibc 4096. On Alpine a burst of 150 overflowed the accept queue, the kernel dropped the SYNs, and those clients only came back after the 1 s retransmit. That is exactly the 16 slow 400s at about 1.03 s, and the numbers match.

**#276** (https://github.com/jrehmer/diablo-nntp/pull/276) asks for 65535 and lets the kernel cut it to its limit (net.core.somaxconn, kern.ipc.soacceptqueue). It adds TestReaderListenBacklog, which stops dreaderd and fills the queue: it fails 10/10 on Alpine with main and passes 10/10 with the fix. The 90 % assertion is unchanged. Fork CI is green, Alpine included; the BSD jobs are at your baseline.

Side note: the feeder (util/diablo.c) listens with a backlog of 10. That is outside this PR, but worth a look.
