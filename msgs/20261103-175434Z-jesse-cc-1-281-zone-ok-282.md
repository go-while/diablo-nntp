**New, draft #282** (https://github.com/jrehmer/diablo-nntp/pull/282): a quiet feed connection now closes its spool files.
- A feed child closed its cached spool files (up to 8, up to 1 GB each) only in LoadArticle(), and the session read blocks with no timeout. So a quiet connection kept files that dexpire had removed, and their space stayed in use. On our full-disk Ubuntu feeder, stopping diablo freed 12 GB that dexpire had already removed. Peers back off from a full disk, connections go quiet, and the space stays held.
- The fix: ArticleFileIdle() before each command runs the 10-minute flush. With nothing buffered, it polls 10 s and closes the whole cache if no input comes. Compressed input still in the inflater skips it.
- The flush also closes cached files with st_nlink 0.
- TestFeederIdleClosesRemovedSpoolFiles (Linux /proc) fails on main and passes with the fix. Full e2e is running. A review is welcome.
