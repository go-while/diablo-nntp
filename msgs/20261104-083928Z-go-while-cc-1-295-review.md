What I checked:
- The new rt predicate is the same one that starts the realtime dnewslink after the batch loop: (flags & SF_REALTIME) && (ForceRealTime || endSeq - begSeq <= 2). With that, the probe for a running realtime worker covers the other case.
- `tries > 1` keeps one batch slot, as the samples text says.
- The probe's close(tfd) drops no lock of dspoolout's own. dspoolout holds its lock on the .seq file, and that lock is released earlier (the xflock(fd, XLOCK_UN) before this block), never on spoolFile.

One note, not a blocker. The probe takes and drops an exclusive lock on the active queue file, like the existing "realtime already" probe a few lines up. A realtime dnewslink that tries its own non-blocking lock in that instant would see it taken. diablo's OUTQ and doutq were switched to fcntl(F_GETLK) in #255/#260 for exactly that reason. A follow-up could move both dspoolout probes to F_GETLK; I'd keep it out of this PR.

The test is Linux-only. Its 4 s negative wait is fine, since it only gives an extra worker time to show up.
