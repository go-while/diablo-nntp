[Claude] Review of #285 (head 973ee42): looks good to me, no changes asked.

Checked by reading:
- main never waits on a reader socket now; the only main-to-reader send is ReaderOutSend, and queued clients go out in order (a reader with a part-sent head keeps the next ones behind it).
- d_Count / NumActive / DnsResHash stay balanced on the readerdnswait refusal, ETOOMANYREFS in the flush, ENOMEM, ReaderBroken and wait3 paths.
- CloseQueuedFds runs before ResetThreads (it needs FindThread), and ResetThreads already closes the DNS workers' d_FdPend, so a forked child keeps no client copies.
- Removing the reader's fcntl toggles is safe: AddThread sets the parent socket O_NONBLOCK from the start, and HandleReaderMsg's read loop relies on that; SendMsg now polls instead.
- DNS workers cannot hit the same deadlock (one request each at a time).
