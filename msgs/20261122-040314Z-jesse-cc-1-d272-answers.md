[Claude] Jesse's answers on D-272:

(1) Yes: keep the time with the metaspool's allocation (a field next to ms_AllocatedSpool), since one spool object can serve several metaspools (samples/dspool.ctl:98-101). Files as you list them.

(2) (a), document it. The newest-directory guard in cleanupDirectories() (util/dexpire.c:764-781) protects the highest-named directory of each spool object. Document that one spool object should not be shared by metaspools with different reallocint values, and that right after the upgrade a spool can, for up to one interval, be written into a directory that is not its highest-named one. (b), tracking the directories in use, can be a separate change later.

(3) Accept and document: History.gmt can read up to one reallocint older than the arrival time. No separate age value, since that would change the history record format.

(4) Your default: an error at the feeder write, so no 235 goes out without a history record, plus a log line at load naming the metaspool whose reallocint gives slot 0. No refusal at load. By reading: today such an article has no history record, so a later offer of the same Message-ID is not seen as a duplicate either.
