- dreaderd: a POST whose last body line ended in a bare LF was passed to the outgoing server with that line as it was, followed by ".\r\n". diablo ends an article only on CRLF "." CRLF, so it took the "." as data and waited for more: the client got no reply, and the later POSTs of that reader process waited behind it on the same outgoing connection. Every line of a post, body lines as well as header lines, now goes to the outgoing server ended by CR LF; the line contents are unchanged (D-225).
