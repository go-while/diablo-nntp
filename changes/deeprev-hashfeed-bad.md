- dnewsfeeds, dspool.ctl, dserver.hosts: a hashfeed entry that cannot be read (no modulus as in `1-2`, `1-2/0`, `abc`, junk after a number, an offset outside 0..12 such as `:13` or `:256`) was accepted and divided by zero in the master on the next article. It is now logged ("bad hashfeed entry"), and the whole hashfeed matches no article: the feed gets nothing, the metaspool takes nothing, the reader asks that server for nothing, and an IFILTER label filters every article. Before, a typo could also have been read as no hashfeed at all.
