- `dreaderd`: a command a client sends right after NEWNEWS (pipelined, `allownewnews yes`) is answered after the final "." of the NEWNEWS list. Before, when one group of the list did not fit one pass of the reader loop (more than about 7 KB of message-ids, or more than 10,000 overview records), the end of that group read the next command and answered it inside the 230 block, and the rest of the list followed it. The end of a NEWNEWS group now only refreshes the session's activity time (for the idle timeout), as before at each group end; the other per-command steps run once, after the final ".". One of them is the session byte limit check, by the maintainer's choice (check it once, after the final "."; a 400 is never sent inside a multi-line reply, RFC 3977 3.2.1; no cut into lists): with a large group and small ones after it, a client past the limit got "400 ... Session byte limit reached." after the large group and after each small one, inside the list, then the ".", another 400 and the close; now it gets the whole list, the "." and then the 400 (commands the client had already sent can still be answered after that 400 until D-245, a separate change, is merged). The access-cache reload also waits for the end of the list, and a client that goes away during a scan that prints nothing is noticed when the list ends (found by reading). Output is still sent as the list grows.
