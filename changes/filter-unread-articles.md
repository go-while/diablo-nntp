- diablo: an article with a NUL byte in it (`rejectartswithnul` is off by default), and an article stored on a compressed spool object (`compresslvl`), did not reach the external spam filter (`feederfilter`): reading it for the filter failed, and the article went to the `nospam` feeds (and the `onlyspam` ones) unjudged, with a log line only in DEBUG builds. Such an article is now sent to the filter by its length, a compressed one inflated first; an article that still cannot be read is logged ("cannot read article ..., not filtered") and passes as before (D-241).
