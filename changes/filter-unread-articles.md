- diablo: an article with a NUL byte in it (`rejectartswithnul` is off by default), and every article stored on a compressed spool object (`compresslvl`), was never sent to the external spam filter (`feederfilter`): reading it for the filter failed, and the article went to the `nospam` feeds (and the `onlyspam` ones) unjudged, without a log line. Such an article is now sent to the filter by its length, a compressed one inflated first. The record is read the way the feeder reads it to serve it: the spool header's own length is skipped, a record without a header is read whole, and a record whose header fails the feeder's checks is not judged. An article that cannot be read is logged ("cannot read article ..., not filtered") and passes as before (D-241).
