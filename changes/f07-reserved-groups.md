- `dreaderd`: in default RFC mode, POST refuses reserved newsgroup names/components with `441 Reserved newsgroup name: <name>`, even when the group exists: first component `example`, whole names `poster` and `junk`, whole components `all` and `ctl`, and first components `to` and `control` for ordinary posts (RFC 5536 3.1.4; RFC 5537 3.5 step 4). Matching is case-insensitive and component-based. Control articles may use `control.*`; ihave/sendme Control articles may use `to.*`, with existing controlpost permissions still enforced. `Followup-To: poster` remains valid. Explicit `rfc5537 no` / `legacytrace yes` retains the old behavior for local agreements.
