- dreaderd: new diablo.config option `readersupersedelock` (`require-auth`, `auth`, `none`, `all`, the values of `readercanlock`) for the removal of the article a `Supersedes:` header replaces; cancels stay under `readercanlock`. Not set (the default), a supersede follows `readercanlock`, so existing configurations do not change. The log names the option in force; `readerxover trackonly` is refused for a supersede mode that needs headers. Documented in samples/diablo.config and README.READER.
