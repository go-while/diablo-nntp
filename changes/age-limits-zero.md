- `feedermaxacceptage 0` (and `diablo -r 0`) turns the check of the age of incoming articles off: articles of any age, in the past or the future, are accepted (the `Date:` header is still required). Before, 0 rejected every article that was not dated to the second, and only a negative value switched the check off by accident. `remember 0` now means no limit as well: `dhisexpire` and `didump -x` keep every expired history entry however old, and, as `remember` sets the default of `feedermaxacceptage`, the age check is off too. Use both for an archive. A negative `remember` still drops all expired entries (`didump -r -1`).
