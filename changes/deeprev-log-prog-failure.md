- logging: when the program of a `|prog` log could not be started (no free descriptor, no process slot, the program cannot be executed), the daemon crashed on its next log line, wrote the log into a pipe nobody read, or with a general log of that kind looped until it crashed. It now reports the failure to syslog, logs there and tries the program again after 10 seconds. A program that cannot be executed no longer starts a chain of forks.
