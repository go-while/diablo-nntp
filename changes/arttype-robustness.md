- `diablo`: four small fixes in the article type detection (`lib/arttype.c`). Headers are matched without regard to case in the second letter (`CONTENT-TYPE`, `CONTROL`: `tolower(*(ptr + 1) == 'o')` had its parenthesis in the wrong place), and white space after the colon of `Content-Type:` and `Content-Transfer-Encoding:` is optional (`Content-Type:text/html`, a tab). An article that becomes a binary on its 9th base64, uuencode or BinHex line, the last one, no longer keeps the `default` bit, which made `arttypes default` match a binary. `classifyLineAsTypes()` no longer reads the byte before the buffer on an empty line. A `Content-Type` whose value is on the next line is still not seen. Tests: `TestArticleTypes` (the header cases and the 9th-line cases).
