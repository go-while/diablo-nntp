- `diablo`: only CRLF "." CRLF ends an article on a feed connection (RFC 3977 section 3.1.1), as in INN. A "." line that the peer ended with a bare LF is body data, and so is a "." after a bare LF (no dot unstuffing there). Before, an article that mixed CRLF and bare-LF lines and had a line of just "." was cut at that line, stored short, and the rest of its body was read as commands: one `500 Syntax error or bad command` per line, and a peer such as innxmit closed the session. A peer that ends articles with a bare-LF "." alone no longer ends them; the session stays open until the connection times out.
