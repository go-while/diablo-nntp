- `dreaderd`: prevent the reader/feed child IPC socketpair from stalling or losing framing under a burst of concurrent `AUTHINFO PASS` logins on FreeBSD. `ForkPipe()` now uses `SOCK_SEQPACKET` (with `SOCK_STREAM` fallback where unsupported) for the reader and feed child pipes as well as the DNS workers, and raises `SO_SNDBUF`/`SO_RCVBUF` on both ends above FreeBSD's 8 KiB `AF_UNIX` default. `SendMsg()` and `RecvMsg()` wait with `poll()` instead of busy-spinning on `EAGAIN`/`ENOBUFS`, complete partial writes and reads on the `SOCK_STREAM` fallback without leaking passed descriptors, and both `main` and `HandleReaderMsg()` drain all queued IPC messages per `select()` wakeup.
