- dreaderd: Cancel-Lock generation (F-01, RFC 8315), like INN's nnrpd. With `canlockadmin` and `canlockuser` secrets in a vserverdef (up to 4 of each, every one makes locks), posts get a Cancel-Lock (sha256 and sha1; `canlocksha1 no` for sha256 only) appended to the poster's own, and a user's own cancel or supersede gets the matching Cancel-Key. `addcanlockuser username|ip|none` in the readerdef says who the user lock is for. Without secrets nothing changes.
