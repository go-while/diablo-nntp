- dreaderd: after the "." of a POST that went to the outgoing server, the client stayed in the article state until the 240 came back. What it sent in the meantime (a pipelined command, a second ".") was read as more lines of the article on its way: a command was posted as body text and never answered, a second "." ran the post again on the buffer in flight (two replies, the buffer freed under the outgoing connection), and a fetch command made a second request for the same client. The client now waits for the outgoing server's answer, as it does for an article fetch; its next lines are read as commands after the 240 or 441. While it waits, the pass over all connections (every 30 seconds) looks at its socket without reading it: a client that closed is let go then, not only when the outgoing server answers, unless its article is being sent at that moment (D-228).
