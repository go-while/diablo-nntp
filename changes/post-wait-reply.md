- dreaderd: after the "." of a POST that went to the outgoing server, the client stayed in the article state until the 240 came back. What it sent in the meantime went to the article code: a pipelined command was posted as body text and not answered, and a second "." ran the post again on the buffer in flight (two replies, the buffer freed under the outgoing connection) and put the client back to commands while its post was still in flight, so a fetch command after it made a second request for the same client. The client now waits for the outgoing server's answer, as it does for an article fetch; its next lines are read as commands after the 240 or 441. While it waits, the pass over all connections (every 30 seconds) looks at its socket without reading it: a client whose connection failed (a reset) is let go then, not only when the outgoing server answers; a client that closed or half-closed cleanly keeps its connection until the answer, so its post is still sent whole (D-228).
