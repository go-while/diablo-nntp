- Feeder Xref slave with `feederxrefsync on`: a group in `Xref:` that is not in dactive.kp is skipped and the NX of the carried groups after it is still synced. With `activedrop on` the article is now refused only when none of the groups on the `Xref:` line is carried, as documented; before, the first missing group ended the sync and the article was refused NotInActive (and the refusal went into history, so the article was lost on this host). An `Xref:` entry whose number is empty, not all digits or too large leaves that group's NX unchanged instead of writing a wrong value. Only the first line of a folded `Xref:` header is read (unchanged).
