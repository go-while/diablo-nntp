- Feeder Xref slave with `feederxrefsync on`: a group in `Xref:` that is not in dactive.kp is skipped and the NX of the carried groups after it is still synced. With `activedrop on` the article is now refused only when none of the groups on the `Xref:` line is carried, as documented; before, the first missing group ended the sync and the article was refused NotInActive (and the refusal went into history, so no other peer could deliver the article here while the history entry lasted). An `Xref:` entry whose number is empty, not all digits or too large (checked after the entry is cut to the group-name buffer, as before) leaves that group's NX unchanged instead of writing a wrong value. Only the first line of a folded `Xref:` header is read (unchanged).
