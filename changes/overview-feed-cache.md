- dreaderd: new option `readerovcache` (default 8, the old fixed size): the number of groups whose overview files a reader or feed process keeps open. A full cache now closes the group used least recently, instead of half of the cache. A feed posting across thousands of groups used to reopen and remap a group's overview index for almost every article (96% of the writes on a test archive fed with an old backfill); `readerovcache 1024` or more keeps most of them open. dreaderd lowers the number to fit the descriptor limit and FD_SETSIZE and logs it; out of descriptors, it closes cached groups and retries the open.
- `KPDBWrite()` writes nothing when a field already holds the new value. dreaderd sharing dactive.kp with diablo no longer rewrites every group's NE with the number diablo just stored.
