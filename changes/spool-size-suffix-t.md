- The `t` (or `T`) size suffix in `dspool.ctl` (spool `minfree` and `maxsize`, metaspool `maxsize`) now means terabytes. Before it was read like `m`, so `maxsize 4t` was 4 MiB, not 4 TiB. **Upgrade effect:** every existing `t` setting becomes 1,048,576 times larger. Spool `minfree 4t` goes from 4 MiB to 4 TiB free space wanted (`dexpire` may then expire aggressively trying to reach it); spool `maxsize 4t` goes from a 4 MiB to a 4 TiB allowance; metaspool `maxsize 4t` is the article-size matching limit, not the spool capacity, and rises the same way. An admin who wrote a `t` value meaning megabytes must change it. This is the intended correction. `k`, `m`, `g` and a plain number (kilobytes) are unchanged.
