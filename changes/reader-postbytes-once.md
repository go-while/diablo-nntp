- dreaderd: an accepted POST was counted twice in the byte count of its session: once as its lines were read, and again, as the stored article, when dreaderd's checks passed. The `postbytes=` of the "closed from" log line, the `PostBytes` of `drcmd stats` and the post bytes a NetRemote server gets at the end of a session (builds with `NETREMOTE_ENABLED`) showed the lines of an accepted post plus the stored article, which also holds the headers dreaderd adds: about twice the size of a large post, more for a small one. A post is now counted once, as it is read: the bytes the client sent after the POST command up to, not including, the "." line, with the line ends and dot-stuffing as sent. A `Bytes:` or `Xref:` header line, which dreaderd drops while reading, and a line over the line limit are not counted, as before. A post that passed dreaderd's checks and was then refused by the server it is posted to, or mailed to a moderator, was counted twice as well and is now counted once. A post refused by dreaderd's checks and a fed article (IHAVE, TAKETHIS) were counted once and still are, and `posts=` is unchanged. `postbytes=` counts the lines received from the client; the size in the "post ok <id> <size>" line of the post log is the stored article, with the headers dreaderd adds.
