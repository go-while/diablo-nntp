- dreaderd: the main process no longer waits for a reader whose socketpair is full. A client on its way to a reader that has no room is queued, with its descriptor, and sent when the reader reads again. Before, a burst of logins could fill both directions between the main process and a reader; each then waited in SendMsg() for the other to read, and dreaderd stopped serving (logins hung, new clients got no greeting) until it was restarted. It showed on illumos, where the socketpairs are SOCK_STREAM (no SOCK_SEQPACKET) with about 16 KiB per direction: 40 AUTHINFO logins at once were enough. With SOCK_SEQPACKET and 256 KiB buffers (Linux, FreeBSD) it takes a much larger burst.
