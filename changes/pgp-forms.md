- `diablo`: every form of PGP in an article gets the `pgpmessage` article type, not only the exact line `-----BEGIN PGP MESSAGE-----`. Before, the multi-part message (`BEGIN PGP MESSAGE, PART 01/02`), the clear-signed message (`BEGIN PGP SIGNED MESSAGE`), a lone signature, and public and private key blocks were `default`; and PGP/MIME (RFC 3156) had no PGP flag, with an encrypted article counted as a binary because of its `application/octet-stream` part. Now any `-----BEGIN PGP ...` line sets the flag, a `Content-Type` of `application/pgp-encrypted`, `-signature` or `-keys` (or a `multipart` line naming one) sets it, and the octet-stream part of an article that carries `application/pgp-encrypted` is not a binary. An `arttypes pgpmessage` or `arttypes !pgpmessage` rule in `dnewsfeeds` sees the same set of articles for all forms. An octet-stream attachment elsewhere is still a binary. Tests: `TestArticleTypes` (the PGP cases).
