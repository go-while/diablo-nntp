- `dreaderd` `cache=scoring`: a reader process no longer dies of SIGFPE when `che_ReadArt` reaches 0, one group keeps one `cache.hits` entry (the key is compared field by field, not with `memcmp` over its padding), and the counters are updated atomically, so concurrent readers and `dexpirescoring` no longer lose each other's counts. `dexpirescoring` checks the scoreboard header like dreaderd, decays the entry that ends exactly at the end of the file, and stops on a damaged or looping chain.
- Feed stats (`feederrtstats`): the incoming connection is counted under the `FSTATS_IN` lock, each host's stats are locked in its own record instead of all hosts sharing locks in the dummy record, and `dfeedinfo -P` read-locks each record for its snapshot.
- Old and new binaries should not run at the same time: restart the readers, `dexpirescoring`, the feeder and the stats tools (`dnewslink`, `dfeedinfo`) together.
