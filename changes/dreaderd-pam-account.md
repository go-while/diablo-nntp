- dreaderd: a PAM login (the `pam <service>` option of an authdef in dreader.access) now runs PAM's account phase (`pam_acct_mgmt()`) after the password check, and passes the client's address to PAM as `PAM_RHOST`. An account that the account phase refuses (expired, disabled, denied by pam_access or pam_time) no longer logs in with its password; it gets 481, and dreaderd logs `PAM account check for <user> from <address>: <reason>, login refused` at LOG_NOTICE. A password that has expired and must be changed (PAM_NEW_AUTHTOK_REQD) is refused the same way, since an NNTP client cannot change it (D-237). This is on by default, with no option. **Check your PAM service before upgrading:** the service is the name after `pam` in the authdef (`nntp` in samples/dreader.access: `/etc/pam.d/nntp`, or the `nntp` lines of `/etc/pam.conf` on systems that use that file, such as illumos). A service file with only `auth` lines has no `account` lines, and the account phase then refuses (Linux-PAM uses the `other` service, which on many systems is pam_deny; OpenPAM fails the phase), so every PAM login of that authdef is refused. Add an `account` line to the service, for example `account required pam_unix.so` to check the system accounts, or `account required pam_permit.so` to keep the old behaviour.
