- `diablo`: a feed connection that goes quiet now closes its cached spool files, so the space of files dexpire has removed is freed. Before, a feed child closed its (up to 8, up to 1 GB each) spool files only when an article arrived. A connection whose peer backed off, for example from a full disk, or that sent only CHECKs, kept removed files open: dexpire freed nothing, and the disk stayed full until the child exited. On a test feeder, stopping diablo freed 12 GB that dexpire had already removed.
  - When no input is buffered and none arrives within 10 seconds, the session closes its cached spool files. A busy feed never waits that long.
  - The 10-minute cache flush also runs before every command, not only for articles, and it also closes any cached file that has been removed.
