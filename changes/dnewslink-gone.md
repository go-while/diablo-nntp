- `dnewslink`: a queued article that has expired from the spool is no longer offered. Before, it was offered and then sent as an empty article (just the `.`), which the peer rejected as a broken article (diablo: 3-byte `TooSmall`) under a good message-id. Now no CHECK, TAKETHIS or IHAVE goes out for it and the entry is dropped for good (`gone=` in the stats). A spool file that is there but cannot be opened (permission, I/O, too many open files) is logged and its entries stay in the queue (`spoolerr=`). An entry that cannot be read as an article (corrupt) is dropped (`bad=`): streaming sends no TAKETHIS for it after a 238 (CHECK replies are advisory, RFC 4644 2.4.2; innfeed does the same), IHAVE mode sends an empty article after the 335, as innfeed does. IHAVE mode still reads an article only after the 335.
- `-x` stats the spool file by its full path. Before, it stat'ed the queue's relative path from dnewslink's working directory, never found it, and so skipped every article. It is now mostly redundant and kept for compatibility.
- The spool file cache drops the least recently used file instead of a random one, finds a cached file behind a freed slot instead of opening it twice, and no longer leaks a descriptor when a compressed article cannot be read.
