- `dnewslink`: a queued article that has expired from the spool is no longer offered. Before, it was offered and then sent as an empty article (just the `.`), which the peer rejected as a broken article (diablo: 3-byte `TooSmall`) under a good message-id. Now dnewslink sends no CHECK, TAKETHIS or IHAVE for it, drops it from the queue for good (not deferred or refiled), and logs how many with the statistics: `N articles expired before they could be sent`.
- If the article cannot be read only after the peer answered 238 to its CHECK, dnewslink sends no TAKETHIS for it (CHECK replies are advisory, RFC 4644 2.4.2; innfeed does the same). In IHAVE mode the article is read before the IHAVE, so the peer never waits after a 335 for an article dnewslink cannot send.
- `-x` stats the spool file by its full path. Before, it stat'ed the queue's relative path from dnewslink's working directory, never found it, and so skipped every article. It is now mostly redundant and kept for compatibility.
