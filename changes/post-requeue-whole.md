- dreaderd: an article on its way to the outgoing server was read out of the client's buffer as it was sent. If the outgoing server answered 335 and then closed, timed out or restarted before its 235, the post was requeued and the next server connection got only the rest of the article, or only the "." line, and could store that. If the client went away while the article was being sent (idle or session timeout, reader shutdown), dreaderd sent the "." line after the part already sent, and the outgoing server stored a cut article. dreaderd now queues the whole article for the outgoing server when it answers 335 and keeps the client's copy until the post is answered: a requeued post is sent whole again, and a client that goes away no longer cuts the article short. The article bytes are unchanged.
