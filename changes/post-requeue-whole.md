- dreaderd: an article on its way to the outgoing server was read out of the client's buffer as it was sent. If the outgoing server answered 335 and then closed, timed out or restarted before its 235, the post was requeued and the next server connection got only the rest of the article, or only the "." line, and could store that. If the client went away while the article was being sent (idle or session timeout), dreaderd sent the "." line after the part already sent, and the outgoing server stored a cut article. dreaderd now queues the whole article for the outgoing server when it answers 335 and keeps the client's copy until the post is answered: a requeued post is sent whole again, and a client that goes away no longer cuts the article short. The article bytes are unchanged. A post whose client went away before its article was sent is no longer offered with a "." alone (an empty article): a post queued behind another one is dropped before its IHAVE, and when the client goes away between the IHAVE and the 335, dreaderd closes the outgoing connection instead (the posts queued behind it go to the next connection). A posting client whose connection is closed cleanly or half-closed (no QUIT) after its complete POST is no longer let go while it waits for the outgoing server's answer: its post is sent and stored, and the client is closed after the answer (a half-closed one still gets it). Only a socket error (a reset) lets such a client go at once, as before for every close. Notes: a client that is ended (idle or session timeout, socket error) before the outgoing server answered 335 has its post dropped and may have seen no reply at all, or only the 400; one ended after the 335 has its article sent whole to the outgoing server. When an outgoing server stores an article but drops the connection before its 235, the requeued post is offered again: the same server then refuses it as a duplicate (the client gets a 441 although the article is stored), or another server stores a second copy, which the Message-ID history of the peers drops. A client whose connection fails (a reset) while its POST waits in dreaderd's queue for an outgoing server (all of them down or busy) is now let go at the next pass over all connections too, not only once a server answers.
