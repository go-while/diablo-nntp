- `dreaderd`: the Date a post gets when it has none is an RFC 5322 date-time in UTC, `Thu, 05 Nov 2026 12:00:00 +0000`, instead of `05 Nov 2026 12:00:00 GMT` (RFC 5536 3.1.1: the obsolete `GMT` zone must not be generated). A poster's Date or Injection-Date is refused with 441 when it cannot be read, is more than 24 hours in the future, or is older than the feeder accepts: `feedermaxacceptage` (by default `remember`, 14 days), but never less than 72 hours; with `feedermaxacceptage 0` only the future is checked (RFC 5537 3.5 step 3; ISSUES.md F-07, step 3). Before, any Date was taken, and the feeder refused an old one later.
