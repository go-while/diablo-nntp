- `dreaderd`: a new vserverdef option `rfc5537` (default `yes`) turns on the RFC 5536/5537 posting behavior of F-07 as one: Injection-Info and Injection-Date in place of NNTP-Posting-Host, X-Trace and X-Complaints-To, `.POSTED` in the Path, the Date window (a poster's Date or Injection-Date that cannot be read, is more than 24 hours ahead or older than `feedermaxacceptage` is refused), and, new, the RFC 5536 3.1.3 Message-ID syntax: `<` dot-atom-text `@` dot-atom-text or `[literal]` `>`, at most 250 octets, no comment, otherwise 441 (RFC 5537 3.5 step 2). With `no`, dreaderd posts as before F-07, with the old trace headers and the lax Message-ID check (ids with `..`, two `@` or a comment are taken, like INN's `laxmid`). **Upgrade note:** v7 enables this behavior by default: a server that wants the old trace headers and lax Date and Message-ID checks must set `rfc5537 no` (or `legacytrace yes`). `legacytrace yes` still works and means `rfc5537 no`. Either way a post with Injection-Info, Xref or `.POSTED` in its Path is refused, and the Date dreaderd adds ends in `+0000`.
- `dreaderd`: Injection-Info gives each datum its own parameter (RFC 5536 3.2.8): `posting-host` is the client's name and address, or with a `cryptpw` the hourly hash NNTP-Posting-Host had (before it went into `posting-account`); `logging-data` is only `<time> <pid>`, to find the post in the logs, with or without a `cryptpw` (before, it was the X-Trace data, account and address included, encrypted with a `cryptpw`). A new vserverdef option `showposter yes` (default no, as INN's `addinjectionpostingaccount`) adds `posting-account="<user>"` for a logged-in user, encrypted with a `cryptpw`. `mail-complaints-to` is still `abuseto`. The Date and Injection-Date dreaderd adds end in `+0000 (UTC)`, as INN writes them.
