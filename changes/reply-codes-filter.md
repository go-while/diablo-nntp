- diablo: the external spam filter's answer (feederfilter) is compared as the whole code (NntpCode()): 335 takes the article, 435 or 437 mark it as spam (cleanfeed in highwind mode answers exactly "335" or "435"). Any other answer is a filter failure: the filter is restarted and the article is not marked as spam. An onlyspam feed now gets only what the filter called spam, not an article the filter failed on (before, a failed filter sent the article to onlyspam feeds too). Before, every answer starting with 4 marked the article as spam and an unknown answer left the filter running out of step.
