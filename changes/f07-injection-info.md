- `dreaderd`: a local post now carries `Injection-Info` (RFC 5536 3.2.8) in place of `NNTP-Posting-Host`, `X-Trace` and `X-Complaints-To`, with the same data: `posting-host` (the client's name and address; with a `cryptpw`, the hourly hash in `posting-account` instead), `logging-data` (the X-Trace data, encrypted with the `cryptpw` as before) and `mail-complaints-to` (`abuseto`). `Injection-Date` is added where RFC 5537 3.5 step 11 wants one (dreaderd supplied the Message-ID or Date) and a poster's own is kept. The Path gets the `.POSTED` diagnostic after the `postpath` (RFC 5537 3.2.1). A post to a moderated group is mailed to the moderator without Injection-Info and without our Injection-Date (RFC 5537 3.5 step 7). The vserverdef option `legacytrace yes` keeps the old behaviour: the old trace headers, and no Injection-* header and no `.POSTED` (ISSUES.md F-07, step 2).
