- build: the allocation tracker (`DEBUG_ZALLOC`) and the mbuf caller notes (`DEBUG_MBUF`) are no longer compiled in by default; they cost time on every allocation and only the SIGUSR2 dump of dreaderd used them. `make clean && make DEBUG_ALLOC="-DDEBUG_ZALLOC -DDEBUG_MBUF"` switches them on (clean also when switching back). Without them dreaderd ignores SIGUSR2 instead of dumping.
