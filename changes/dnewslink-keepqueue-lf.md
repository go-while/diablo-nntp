- dnewslink: a queue line of 1024 bytes or more (with its newline) that is kept in the queue after a spool error, or copied with the unprocessed rest of the queue when dnewslink stops early, now ends in a newline, so the entry behind it is no longer glued onto it and lost at the next run. The part of such a line beyond 1023 bytes is still dropped by the reader, as before. The deferred-entry and refile writers use the same helper.
