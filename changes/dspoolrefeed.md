- New `dspoolrefeed`: writes dnewslink queue files for every article on the spool, to feed a whole spool to another server (a new server, or one that lost its spool). It reads the history, takes each Message-ID from the article on the spool (compressed spool objects too) and checks it against the history hash, and leaves out expired, header-only and vanished articles. `-a days` and `-g groups` limit it; `-n` splits the output for parallel dnewslinks. See dspoolrefeed(8) (ISSUES.md F-03).
