- diablo: a `HEAD`, `BODY` or `ARTICLE <id> a<N>` request for an article older than N seconds ("430 Article prohibited") now releases the article it opened, as the other answers do. Before, each such request left one mapping (or, on a compressed spool, one heap copy of the article) in the connection's diablo process; dreaderd passes a reader's own `a<N>` through, so a reader could repeat it until the map limit made every fetch on that spool connection answer 430 for stored articles (D-227).
