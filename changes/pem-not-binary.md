- `diablo`: the lines of a PEM block (`-----BEGIN CERTIFICATE-----`, `RSA PRIVATE KEY`, `PUBLIC KEY`, `OPENSSH PRIVATE KEY` and the like, up to the matching `-----END ...-----`) are no longer counted as base64 when the article type is worked out, as the lines of a PGP block already are. A certificate has more than 8 lines of 64 base64 characters, so a text post with a certificate or key (a TLS or ssh help request) was classified binary and refused by the sample `IFILTER` (`delgroup *.bina*`) outside binary groups. The first 100 lines of a block are skipped, so a long binary can't hide behind a `BEGIN` line. Only the `BEGIN PGP` forms get the `pgpmessage` type. Tests: `TestArticleTypes` (the PEM cases), `TestPEMArticleNotRejectedAsBinary`.
