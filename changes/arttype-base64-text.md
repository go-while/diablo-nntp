- A text part sent as base64 (`Content-Type: text/*`, or no Content-Type, with `Content-Transfer-Encoding: base64`) is no longer taken for a binary just because of its encoding. This is common in mail gateways such as the linux.* hierarchy: patch mails with a UTF-8 body arrived as base64 and were refused as a binary in a non-binary group (`IncomingFilter`, ArtType 000b00). `lib/arttype.c` now decodes each base64 line of such a body and calls the article binary only when it decodes to something that is not text (a NUL or a control character other than TAB, LF, CR, FF and ESC). Base64 with any other Content-Type (image, application, multipart parts) is binary as before, and so is a long run of base64 lines in a text part that does not decode to text.
