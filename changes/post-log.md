- `dreaderd`: a new diablo.config option `path_postlog` (for example `%s/post.log`) gives the post lines a log of their own; without it they stay in the general log, as before, and `NONE` drops them. Refused posts are now logged too, as `<user>/<host> (<address>) post refused <msgid> <reply>`, with `-` for a post that has no Message-ID yet: refused by dreaderd's own checks, POST not allowed (440), the moderator mail failing, or the server the post goes to refusing it at IHAVE or after the article. Such a line can follow a "post ok" line, which is written when dreaderd's own checks pass. `adm/daily.atrim` rotates `post.log` with the other daemon logs.
