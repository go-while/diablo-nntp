- A line longer than the read buffer (255 characters in `diablo.config`, 511 in `dspool.ctl` and `dexpire.ctl`) is no longer split into two lines. A long comment, or a long line whose cut part follows a `#`, is read as before. Any other long line is reported: `diablo.config` and `dspool.ctl` stop with an error (before, the line was silently split and its tail read as another line), and `dexpire.ctl` ignores it with a log line. A `dexpire.ctl` group pattern longer than 254 characters is refused instead of overrunning its 255-byte field (D-82).
