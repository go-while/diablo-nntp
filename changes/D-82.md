- A line longer than the read buffer in `diablo.config`, `dspool.ctl` or `dexpire.ctl` is no longer split into two lines: a long comment is skipped, a long command is reported (`diablo.config`, `dspool.ctl`: error; `dexpire.ctl`: ignored with a log line). A `dexpire.ctl` group pattern longer than 254 characters is refused instead of overrunning its 255-byte field (D-82).
