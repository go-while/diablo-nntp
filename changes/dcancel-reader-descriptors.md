- dcancel: `dcancel -r` no longer runs out of descriptors on a long list. It opened the reader active file for every input line, and for a group in active also the group's overview index, and kept them open and mapped, so with a limit of 1024 descriptors it stopped with "Unable to open active file" after about 500 lines of existing groups. The active file is now opened once per run, and the overview index is unmapped and closed after each line (D-286).
