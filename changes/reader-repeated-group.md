- dreaderd: an article that names a group more than once in Newsgroups (`a,b,a`) now gets one article number and one overview record in that group, and one entry per group in the Xref line dreaderd adds. Before, every repeat took a number and wrote a record; 512 or more repeats of one group (the default index size) overwrote the index slots of that group's older articles, which then answered 423 and were missing from OVER. The stored header is unchanged. D-226.
