- dreaderd: a checkgroups creates a group, or changes its moderated flag, only where a newgroup of that group from the same sender would be executed, and removes a group only where an rmgroup would (the last matching newgroup/rmgroup or `all` line of dcontrol.ctl must be `doit`, or `verify-<key>` with the key the checkgroups line was verified with). So `newgroup:*:*:drop` and `rmgroup:*:*:drop` now also keep checkgroups from creating and removing groups. The changes not made are written to the control log as dgrpctl commands; descriptions are still updated. The shipped dcontrol.ctl samples are not affected; a dcontrol.ctl that has checkgroups lines but no newgroup/rmgroup lines for a hierarchy no longer creates or removes groups there by checkgroups. INN does not make this check (D-200).
