- `dreaderd`: with the reader cache on (`readercache`, on by default), a reader whose age limit is in force (readerdef `maxagepolicy`, or its own `a<N>` after the message-id) is no longer served an article from the cache; the request goes to the spool server, which applies the limit, as when the article is not cached. Before, once a reader without that limit had fetched an article into the cache, a reader with the limit got it from there (ARTICLE, HEAD, BODY and STAT by message-id, ARTICLE and BODY by number) although the spool server refuses it to them. Readers with a limit now always ask the spool server; readers without one are served from the cache as before.
