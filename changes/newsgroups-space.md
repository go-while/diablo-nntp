- `diablo`: a `Newsgroups:` header with a space after the comma (`alt.binaries.test, alt.binaries.boneless`) is read like one without. The incoming filter (`IFILTER` in `dnewsfeeds`), the feed group match and the `requiregroups` check stepped past the comma but not the space, so every group after the first was matched as an empty name and an article could be rejected as IncomingFilter that the filter should not have matched; with `activedrop yes`, `FirstModeratedGroup()` (and `InActive()`, without an xrefhost) stopped at the first `, ` and never looked at the groups after it, so an unapproved article to `open.group, moderated.group` was accepted. An empty entry in the list (a trailing comma, which some posting tools write, or `,,`) is no longer a group: it was matched as the empty name, so the sample `IFILTER` (`addgroup *`, `delgroup *.bina*`) rejected a binary article whose `Newsgroups:` ended in a comma, and it counted for `mincross`/`maxcross`. INN's `NGsplit` ignores empty entries too. Tests: `TestNewsgroupsSpaceAfterComma`, `TestNewsgroupsSpaceActive`, `TestNewsgroupsEmptyEntry*`.
