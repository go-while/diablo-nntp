- `dreaderd`: a lookup by Message-ID in the overview (`XHDR`, `XOVER`, `HDR` and `OVER` with a Message-ID) and a cancel now compare the `Message-ID:` header kept in the overview data with the requested Message-ID, and skip an entry that has another one. The index keeps only the hash of the Message-ID, and with `msgidhash hash` (the default) two Message-IDs can share it (D-210; `<1510900044@gn>` and `<1510900045@gn>` are such a pair): in one group a lookup, or a cancel, of one of them took the other's article (D-211, its overview half). An entry whose header is not kept or cannot be read (`readerxover trackonly`, older than the group's age limit) still matches a cancel by its hash, as before; for a lookup it counts as not found. The spool lookups and the pre-cancel cache are not changed here.
