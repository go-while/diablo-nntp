- `dreaderd`: a readerdef `groups` list now also limits what a reader may fetch by message-id (D-195), as INN's `PERMartok()` does. ARTICLE, HEAD, BODY, STAT and HDR/XHDR `<message-id>` of an article in none of the reader's groups (taken from Xref, else Newsgroups) answer `502 Read access denied for this article`; a crosspost to an allowed group and an article with neither header are readable, an unknown id stays 430. Before, `groups` only limited GROUP and LISTGROUP, and any article could be read by its message-id. Readers without a `groups` list are not affected.
