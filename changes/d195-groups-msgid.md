- `dreaderd`: a readerdef `groups` list now also limits what a reader may fetch by message-id (D-195), as INN's `PERMartok()` does. ARTICLE, HEAD, BODY, STAT and HDR/XHDR `<message-id>` of an article in none of the reader's groups (taken from Xref, else Newsgroups) answer `480 Read access denied for this article` before a login and `502` after one, as INN; a crosspost to an allowed group and an article with neither header are readable, an unknown id stays 430. Before, `groups` only limited GROUP and LISTGROUP, and any article could be read by its message-id. Readers without a `groups` list are not affected.
- `dreaderd`: BODY by message-id answers `222 0 <message-id>` as RFC 3977 6.2.3 requires (D-196); when the body came from the spool server, it answered the number of the current article of the selected group.
