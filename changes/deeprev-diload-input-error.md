- diload: exits non-zero after a line it cannot read or a read error, instead of reporting success for a history that ends at the bad line; a DUMP line that stops inside a field (after boff=, in file= or flags=) or has an unknown field is such a line now. Input without the final "." line is still accepted, and so are old-style (didump -o) dumps, lists without flags=, mixed lists and lines ending in CR LF.
