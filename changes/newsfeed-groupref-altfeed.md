- dnewsfeeds: a label with a peer feed table (`feeds/<label>`, written by feedrset/feedadd/feedcommit) keeps the `requiregroup` lines it has in dnewsfeeds; the table emptied that list, so articles without a required group were fed to that peer, and a `requiregroup` in the table replaced the label's own ones instead of counting beside them (D-261). Such a peer now gets fewer articles: only those its requiregroup lines let through.
- dnewsfeeds: a `groupref` in the `IFILTER`, `ISPAM` and `ESPAM` labels takes effect; it was silently ignored (D-262). A site that has one now refuses (IFILTER) or spam-checks (ISPAM, ESPAM) the articles of those groups. Only the group lists are taken from the groupdef: its simple options (`maxcross`, `maxsize` and so on) still do not apply to these three labels.
- dnewsfeeds: the GLOBAL label is applied once, before the label's own lines, as documented. It was applied again at the start of every `groupref`, so it overrode what the label had set before that groupref: with GLOBAL `delgroup junk.*`, a label `addgroup junk.wanted` followed by a groupref did not get junk.wanted, and with GLOBAL `addgroup *`, a label's `delgroup` before a groupref was undone (D-263). A label that has lines before a groupref which GLOBAL contradicts now gets what those lines say. This also applies to `filter`/`nofilter` and the other per-label lists. A `groupref` in GLOBAL itself no longer recurses until the limit and logs "Infinite recursion in dnewsfeeds file!".
- dnewsfeeds: `nortflush` and `domismatch` work where char is unsigned (arm64, ppc64le, s390x); there they did the opposite (the queue file was flushed per line, the Path: alias check was off) (D-266).
