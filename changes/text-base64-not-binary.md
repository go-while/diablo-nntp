- `diablo`: a base64 `Content-Transfer-Encoding` no longer makes an article a binary when its `Content-Type` is `text/...`. Some clients send non-ASCII text that way, and the header alone marked a three-line message binary (refused by the sample `IFILTER` outside binary groups). The type is settled at the blank line that ends the header block (the two headers can come in either order, also in a MIME part); a part without a `Content-Type`, or an image, audio or other type, is still a binary, and a long base64 body is still a binary by its lines, so a binary can't pass as text. Tests: `TestArticleTypes` (the base64 text cases), `TestBase64TextNotRejectedAsBinary`.
