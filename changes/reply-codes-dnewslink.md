- dnewslink: a reply code is the whole three-digit code followed by a space or the end of the line (NntpCode()); a reply line without one fails the connection, as EOF does. A reply to MODE STREAM or MODE COMPRESS that is neither the expected code nor a one-line refusal (480, 500-503; 403 for COMPRESS, as Diablo's feeder sends) closes the connection instead of going on without the mode on the same stream, where the lines after it were read as the next reply.
- dnewslink: a 431 to TAKETHIS (Diablo's feeder sends it when its history is busy) defers the article once, as a 431 to CHECK does, instead of rejecting it at once; a second 431 while the deferred articles are sent rejects it. Each pending article now keeps whether it is a deferred one sent again, because its reply can come after the defer replay has ended: before, a second 431 to CHECK there deferred the article again, and it was offered again without end; now it counts as 238, as intended. 437 to an IHAVE offer or to TAKETHIS is still taken as a reject.
