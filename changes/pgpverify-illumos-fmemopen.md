- `pgpverify` could not verify a real control message on illumos/SmartOS: it read the article through `fmemopen()`, which repeats the start of the buffer once the stream is about 1 KB in there, so the headers were read twice and the article was refused as having a duplicate `X-PGP-Sig` or signed header (exit status 3). The article is now read from memory with a small line reader of our own. Found by the tests with real ISC control messages (`TestISCControl*`).
