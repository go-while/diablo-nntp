- `diablo`: the lines of an ASCII-armored PGP block (`-----BEGIN PGP ...` to `-----END PGP ...`) are no longer counted as base64, uuencode or BinHex when the article type is worked out. More than 8 lines of 64 base64 characters in a row made an article a binary, and the signature of an RSA-4096 key has 12, so a clear-signed text article (the NoCeM notices, signed control messages) was classified binary and refused by the sample `IFILTER` (`delgroup *.bina*`) outside binary groups, and routed to `arttypes binary` feeds. The first 100 lines of a block are skipped, so a long binary can't hide behind a `BEGIN PGP` line. The PGP flag itself is unchanged (only `BEGIN PGP MESSAGE` sets it). Tests: `TestArticleTypes` (the long armor cases), `TestArticleTypesRoutedByArttypes`, `TestSignedArticleNotRejectedAsBinary`.
