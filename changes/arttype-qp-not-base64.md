- Quoted-printable text in a non-Latin script (Chinese, Japanese, Cyrillic, ...) was taken for a base64 binary and refused (`IncomingFilter`, ArtType 000b00). Its lines (`=E5=9C=A8=E5=A4=A9...=`, 76 characters with a soft line break at the end) are made of base64 characters, and nine in a row make a base64 binary. `lib/arttype.c` now takes a line with an `=` anywhere but in its last two characters for not base64, since base64 uses `=` only as padding at the end of the data. Real base64, with or without padding, is binary as before.
