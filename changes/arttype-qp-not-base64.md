- Quoted-printable text in a non-Latin script (Chinese, Japanese, Cyrillic, ...) was taken for a base64 binary and refused (`IncomingFilter`, ArtType 000b00). Its lines (`=E5=9C=A8...=`, 76 characters with a soft line break at the end) are made of base64 characters, and nine in a row make a base64 binary. `lib/arttype.c` no longer counts such a line of a text part (`text/...`, or no Content-Type) as base64: every `=` in it starts an `=HH` escape (hex, upper or lower case) of a text byte or is the soft line break at its end, and the escapes make up at least half of the line. In a part declared `Content-Transfer-Encoding: quoted-printable` in its header block, one escape or a soft line break is enough, and an escape of a byte that is not text (`=00` to `=1F` but `=09`, `=0A`, `=0C`, `=0D` and `=1B`; `=7F`) makes it a binary, as a decoded byte does in a base64 text part. In an image, application or other non-text part, quoted-printable lines count as base64 lines, declared or not. Base64 has `=` only as padding at the end of the data, so real base64 is binary as before, with or without padding, with a stray `=` in its lines, and as lines without `=` in a part declared quoted-printable.
