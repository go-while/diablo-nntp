- Quoted-printable text in a non-Latin script (Chinese, Japanese, Cyrillic, ...) was taken for a base64 binary and refused (`IncomingFilter`, ArtType 000b00). Its lines (`=E5=9C=A8...=`, 76 characters with a soft line break at the end) are made of base64 characters, and nine in a row make a base64 binary. `lib/arttype.c` no longer counts a quoted-printable line as base64: every `=` in it starts an `=HH` escape (upper-case hex) or is the soft line break at its end, and the escapes make up at least half of the line. In a part declared `Content-Transfer-Encoding: quoted-printable` (in the article's headers or a MIME part's, up to the next `--` line) one escape or a soft line break is enough. Base64 has `=` only as padding at the end of the data, so real base64 is binary as before, with or without padding, with a stray `=` in its lines, and as lines without `=` in a part declared quoted-printable.
