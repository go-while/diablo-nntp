- `diablo`, `dreaderd`: white space between two names in a `Newsgroups:` header separates them like a comma (RFC 5536 3.1.4 allows white space only around the commas). The feeder stopped reading the list at the first such space, so `x a,b` was refused NotInActive although `b` is in the active file; dreaderd cut each comma entry at its first space, so the article was not filed under the groups after it. dreaderd's POST refuses such a list with 441 Invalid newsgroup(s) line, and checks the groups of a list with white space before a comma by name ("a ,b" was checked as "a " and refused). Tests: `TestNewsgroupsSpaceInsideList`, `TestReaderNewsgroupsSpace`, `TestReaderPostNewsgroupsSpace` (D-206).
