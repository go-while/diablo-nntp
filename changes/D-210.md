- New option `msgidhash siphash` in diablo.config: Message-IDs are hashed with SipHash-2-4 (fixed key) in the history, the reader overview and the reader caches. With the default `hash crc`, two Message-IDs of the same length that differ only in one bit of the 5th character from the end (such as `<1510900044@gn>` and `<1510900045@gn>`) often got the same hash, and the second one was refused as a duplicate. SipHash is also about three times faster than crc. The default stays `msgidhash hash`, so nothing changes until a site sets it. Switching needs a new history (`diloadfromspool`, or feeding the server again) and a new reader overview, and the reader caches emptied. A history written with `siphash` is version 3, which older Diablo versions refuse. A history of the other method is refused with a message. A new history has no entries for articles already gone from the spool, so such an article offered again before the `remember` time is over is accepted again (D-210).
