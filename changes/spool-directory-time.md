- diablo: an article is now stored under the directory time of its own metaspool's allocation. Before, one global time (the last metaspool's) was used for all, so with metaspools of different `reallocint` values an article could be filed in the time slot of another metaspool, and the history `gmt` of the record said so too. A directory time of 0 (only with a clock within about one `reallocint` of 1970) is logged naming the metaspool, and the feeder answers with a try-again reply (436 for IHAVE, 431 for TAKETHIS; not stored, no history record) for articles of that metaspool. Do not share one spool object between metaspools with different `reallocint` values: dexpire protects only the highest-named directory of each spool object, and right after this upgrade a spool can, for up to one `reallocint`, be written into a directory that is not its highest-named one. The history `gmt` of an article can be up to one `reallocint` older than its arrival time.
